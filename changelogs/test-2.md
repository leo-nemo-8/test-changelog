Dear valued customer,

We want to inform you that Sixfab CORE will no longer be available starting from **December 31, 2025**. After this date, users with the Raspberry Pi / NVIDIA Jetson Nano 4G/LTE Cellular Modem Kit will no longer be able to install or use Sixfab CORE on their devices.

# Alternative Solutions

To set up cellular communication on your devices, we recommend following the <a href="https://docs.sixfab.com/page/cellular-internet-connection-in-ecm-mode"  target="_blank">ECM (Ethernet Control Model)</a> or <a href="https://docs.sixfab.com/page/setting-up-a-data-connection-over-qmi-interface-using-libqmi"  target="_blank">QMI (Qualcomm MSM Interface)</a> tutorials available on our website. These tutorials will guide you through the process of establishing a reliable cellular connection without the use of Sixfab CORE.

# Support

We understand this change may affect your current setup, and we are here to assist you during the transition. If you have any questions or need support, please don't hesitate to <a href="https://sixfab.com/contact/"  target="_blank">contact us</a>.

Thank you for your understanding during this transition. We remain committed to helping you with future projects and are always here to support you.
